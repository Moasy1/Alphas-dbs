## 2. Monthly Revenue Financial Cascade (ALPHAS v4.1 Rules)

Under ALPHAS v4.1 financial rules, every EGP of gross revenue cascades down through 5 locked accounts:

$$
\text{Gross Revenue (100\%)} \longrightarrow \text{COGS Cap (40\%)} + \text{Closer Fee (15\%)} + \text{Operating Profit (45\%)}
$$

$$
\text{Operating Profit (45\%)} \longrightarrow \text{Capital Reserve (25\% of Op Profit)} + \text{Distributable Profit (75\% of Op Profit)}
$$

## 3. Break-Even Analysis & Overhead Structure

### Fixed Monthly Overheads (EGP 28,000 / mo Baseline)

To keep the agency lean, fixed operational overheads are kept strictly under control:

* **Software & AI Subscriptions (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Figma, Vercel, GSuite):** EGP 6,500 / mo
* **Hosting, Staging Infrastructure & Server-Side Pixel (CAPI):** EGP 4,500 / mo
* **CRM, EmailJS & Automation Subscriptions:** EGP 3,000 / mo
* **Legal, Accounting & Banking Fees:** EGP 4,000 / mo
* **Paid Ads Self-Marketing Budget (Inbound Lead Gen):** EGP 10,000 / mo
* **TOTAL FIXED MONTHLY OVERHEAD:** **EGP 28,000 / mo**

### Break-Even Volume Calculation:

At a 45% Gross Operating Profit margin, the agency breaks even on fixed operational overheads at:

$$
\text{Break-Even Gross Revenue} = \frac{\text{Fixed Overhead}}{\text{Gross Operating Profit \%}} = \frac{\text{EGP 28,000}}{0.45} = \mathbf{EGP\ 62,222\ / \text{month}}
$$

* **Minimum Monthly Deal Volume to Break Even (EGP 62,222/mo):**
  * Option A: 2 × Ecommerce Web Builds (EGP 34,500 ea) = **EGP 69,000**
  * Option B: 4 × Essential Media Buying Retainers (EGP 11,500 ea) + 1 × SMM Growth (EGP 11,592) + 1 × Strategic Audit (EGP 17,250) = **EGP 74,842**
  * Option C: 1 × Enterprise Strategy (EGP 23,000) + 1 × Ecommerce Web (EGP 34,500) + 1 × Starter Ads (EGP 5,750) = **EGP 63,250**

## 7. Deal Pipeline Stages & Execution Checklist

### Pipeline Stages in ALPHAS CRM:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                             PIPELINE STAGES                                 │
├──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬─────────────────┤
│ 1. Lead IN   │ 2. Audit     │ 3. Quote     │ 4. Contract  │ 5. Closed Won   │
│ (New Lead)   │ (Discovery)  │ (Proposal)   │ (Negotiate)  │ (Onboarded)     │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

1. **Lead IN (New Prospect):**
   * Lead record created in Firestore/CRM (`firebase-crm.js`).
   * Initial contact made via WhatsApp / Call within 15 minutes.
2. **Audit & Discovery Session:**
   * Business requirements gathered.
   * Technical / Growth audit performed.
3. **Quotation / Proposal Sent:**
   * Customized proposal generated via `QuotationMaker.jsx`.
   * Dynamic 40% COGS task sub-panels attached.
4. **Contract Negotiation & Scope Lock:**
   * Contract terms reviewed and signed.
   * 15% Closer Fee reserved in sales ledger for the closing partner.
5. **Closed Won (Project Delivery):**
   * Upfront 50% deposit received.
   * Project kickoff meeting scheduled with dev/media lead.
