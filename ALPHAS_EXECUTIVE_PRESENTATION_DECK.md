## 💰 SLIDE 3: The Business Model & Financial Architecture (v4.1)

Every Egyptian Pound (100%) of Gross Revenue cascades automatically through 5 locked accounts:

$$\text{Gross Revenue (100\%)} \longrightarrow \text{COGS Cap (40\%)} + \text{Closer Fee (15\%)} + \text{Gross Operating Profit (45\%)}$$

$$\text{Operating Profit (45\%)} \longrightarrow \text{Capital Reserve (25\% of Op Profit)} + \text{Distributable Net Profit (75\% of Op Profit)}$$

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     REVENUE ALLOCATION BREAKDOWN (100%)                     │
├──────────────────┬───────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────┤
│  COGS (Max 40%)  │ Sales Commission (15%)│  Gross Operating Profit (45%)    │
│  Execution & QA  │ Deal Closer Fee       │  Net Profit Pool & Reserves      │
└──────────────────┴───────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **40% COGS Execution Cap:** Direct budget for dev, media, software tools & freelancers.
2. **15% Sales Closer Fee:** Direct commission paid to whoever closes the deal.
3. **45% Gross Operating Profit:** 25% retained for capital reserves (11.25% of gross), 75% distributed as partner net profit (33.75% of gross).

## 📑 SLIDE 11: Team Terms, Payment Rules & QA SLA

### Operational & Payment Terms:

1. **Standalone Mini-Gigs (GIG-001 to GIG-100):** Require **100% upfront payment** prior to execution kickoff.
2. **Major Web Builds & Bundles:** Require **50% deposit upfront** and 50% prior to production migration.
3. **Staging Environment Rule:** Zero live editing on client production servers; all dev work takes place on ALPHAS staging environments first.

5. **Contractor SLA Penalty:** External freelancers delivering late without notice incur a 10%/day fee deduction from their project budget.
