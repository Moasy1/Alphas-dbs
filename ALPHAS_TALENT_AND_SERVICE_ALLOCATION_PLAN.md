## 📋 7. Freelance Onboarding & Quality Assurance Playbook

To ensure external contractors deliver up to ALPHAS standards, Talent Acquisition enforces a 3-step vetting pipeline:

1. **Portfolio & Technical Sprint Test:**
   * Freelancers must complete a paid mini-gig sprint (e.g., `GIG-046` 5 Reels Pack or `GIG-001` Speed Opt) to verify speed, adherence to design tokens, and communication.
2. **Standardized NDA & Scope Agreement:**
   * All contractors sign an NDA and client ownership agreement before receiving project assets.
3. **SLA Penalty Clause:**
   * Contractors who deliver late without prior notice suffer a 10% per day fee reduction from their project COGS allocation.
